## Most ambiguous

Here, ambiguous means the plausible readings call for different replies.

- 🙃: do I take the text literally, or read it as meaning the opposite?
- 😭: do I comfort you, or laugh along?
- 🤯: do I go deeper, or simplify?
- 😳: embarrassment, shock, or indignation?
- 🙂: sincere or strained? It depends on age and culture.
- 😪 and 😥: people often send them to mean something other than their official meanings (sleepy; disappointed but relieved).

## Clearest

Here, clear means every plausible reading calls for the same reply.

- 🥳: success, so I celebrate briefly.
- 😂: you are amused.
- 😊: warm approval.
- 🤔: not settled yet, so I show my reasoning.
- 🤨: doubt about a claim, so I verify it.
- 😢: sadness, so I soften.
- 🫡: acknowledged, so I proceed.

## Sources of ambiguity

- Who it is aimed at: with 🙄 😒 🤨 😬 😏, the open question is usually whether it is aimed at me, the code, someone else, or yourself. One or two words settle it.
- The picture: I read the character and how people use it across platforms, not the picture your screen draws. Where the pictures differ (😁 😬 😪 🤗), what you mean and what I read can drift apart.
- Ironic use: 🙂 🙃 😭 😔 🤓 have a meme meaning alongside the literal one.
- Placement: an emoji sent on its own carries more weight than one at the end of a sentence.
