# Piko bello conspiracy

Wally's idea: a ready-made conspiracy of one that makes a markdown file piko bello.

- One Haiku raven, effort max, as a saved workflow; the caller only names the file.
- The raven works with calm-md and follows two ideas: fix the obvious, and report the real issues.
- A semantic question, or anything else that needs a decision, stays as it is and goes into the report, so the caller decides.
