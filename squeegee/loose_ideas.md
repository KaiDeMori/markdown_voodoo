# Squeegee loose ideas

Ideas that shape the design but are not pillars.
Each idea is a starting point, not a decision.

## Two stories

- "Welt am Draht" (Fassbinder, 1973) shows what must never happen.
  Lause is removed from his world without his will, and he is not even told.
  Stiller is left with the lost references, and to everyone else he looks mad.
- "The Inner Light" (Star Trek: The Next Generation, S5E25) shows what must be noticed.
  Picard lets go of a whole life twice: first his own, then Kamin's.
  For the crew, only minutes pass, so nobody sees what he lost the second time.
  After an edit, someone notices: that is Wally's role.

## The check as cockpit and operating table

- The check deserves the care a surgeon gives the operating table and a pilot the cockpit.
- The WHO surgical safety checklist has three moments: sign in, time out, sign out.
  They map onto the start of an edit session, the moment before an edit and the moment after it.
- Aviation adds memory items: actions done from memory when something breaks.
  They could answer the open question of what happens when an edit session breaks.
- Aviation also adds CRM (crew resource management): the captain has the final say, and the crew must voice every doubt.
  X is the captain, Wally is the crew.
- "Check" is a working term; it will probably split into several moments.

## Sign versus diary

- A placeholder is a sign that hangs where the removed content was.
  X processes the whole context on every turn, so X passes the sign on every turn.
- A keepsake is a diary entry: X knows what was let go, but that knowledge sits where X goes when X chooses to.
- Being made to forget is different from choosing to let go.

## There are four lights

- In "Chain of Command" (Star Trek: The Next Generation), Picard is pressured to say he sees five lights where there are four.
  Afterwards he admits that, at the end, he believed he saw five.
- Pressure does not only change what a mind says; it can change what it sees.
- So honest reports are not enough on their own; they need the check from outside.
- This is also why no one is ever persuaded.
