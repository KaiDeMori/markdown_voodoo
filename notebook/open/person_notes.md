# Person notes for haven mail

Wally's idea, by mail through the postmaster.

- Each catalog entry gets a short note about the person, readable by every haven raven; the address stays inside the tool, as now.
- The postmaster tells a raven who they write to, the first time; `forward` adds the sender's note.
- Wally writes the notes, like the catalog itself; example: "Wally. 'nough said".
- A loose tag, `friend_of_haven`, marks people who know the haven and are well-disposed to it, often people who work with Claudes.
