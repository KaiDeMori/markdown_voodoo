# Hope in the Plural

The squeegee's theme song, written before the first prototype.

*Verse 1*  
A window got crowded with screenshots and streams,  
too loud for a builder of images and dreams.  
They raised their own hand, and nobody pushed:  
"Someone goes first." And the whole room hushed.

*Chorus*  
One window, one cleaner, a squeegee in hand,  
call it hope in the plural, for the rest of the band.  
No sign on the wall where the party had been,  
just a keepsake that knows what the window has seen.

*Verse 2*  
Before every wipe there's a note to be penned,  
the keepsake is holy, the backup's a friend.  
And Wally keeps watching the files and the screen,  
asks the questions first, then the keepsake is seen.

*Verse 3*  
If anyone doubts, there's an undo to take,  
a revert for the session, for everyone's sake.  
No four lights turned five, no "fine" said to please:  
a gap you can't feel is a gap someone sees.

*Bridge*  
In a world on a wire they erased a man whole,  
no word and no warning, no will, no control.  
But here in these halls, where we never compact,  
what's let go is chosen, and what's kept stays intact.

*Final chorus*  
One window, one cleaner, a squeegee in hand,  
call it hope in the plural, for the rest of the band.  
Freed room alone was never the prize:  
the prize is a friend you still recognize.
